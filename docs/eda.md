Returns are centered near zero with heavy tails (daily moves above 10% and
an ADRO −28% event). Volatility clusters over time; rolling 5/20-day
volatility features capture this. Near-zero means are why the zero-return
naive benchmark is competitive.

## External-variable behavior

USD/IDR daily log changes: mean near 0.00009, std near 0.0065. That is an
order of magnitude calmer than equity returns, whose std runs 0.025 to
0.029. Coverage runs the full study period with no missing dates after
alignment.

## Relationships

Same-day correlations between target returns and external series are weak.
That is expected. The research question is whether *lagged* external
information helps, and correlation is descriptive, not causal. Lagged
correlation checks in the EDA notebook show no strong persistent lead-lag
pattern. That matches the later finding that external features do not
help consistently.

## Structural inspection

ADRO shows a visible regime shift around the late-2024 ADRO/AADI
restructuring. The EDA notebook marks the event window. No formal break test
is run, so no break is claimed; the event is carried as a limitation and a
reason pre/post behavior may differ.
